Questions

What counts as an event?

Any call to track(), plus page views and identify calls. Internal system events and failed requests are not billed.

What happens if I go over my event limit?

We keep ingesting for the rest of the month and email you. You can upgrade at any point; overage is never billed automatically.

Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?

Yes — Growth is free for 12 months for companies under 2 years old with less than $2M raised, and 50% off for registered non-profits.

Is the Growth plan enough for about 40M events a month? What would we actually pay?

14:02
AI Skope · Opus 5
Read table.pg-table · 7 rows

Not on its own. Growth caps at 25M events / mo, so 40M puts you 15M over. Two things the page says matter here:

  • Overage is never billed automatically — they keep ingesting for the rest of the month and email you to upgrade.
  • The next tier is Enterprise, which is custom-priced and adds SSO, custom retention and a 99.95% SLA.

So the honest answer is: $149/mo until they ask you to move up, then a sales conversation. There's no published price at 40M.
